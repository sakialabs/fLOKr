# Product Vision

fLOKr is the main product going forward: a community operating system for newcomers, mutual aid, local action, shared resources, and fair work.

The product should feel practical and warm. It helps people borrow what they need, find trusted helpers, join local circles, coordinate crews, and show up for nearby moves.

## Core Promise

fLOKr helps a local hub turn care into coordinated action:

- people can ask for help or offer support through Loop Signals
- Circles give recurring groups a clear home
- Crews coordinate practical action
- Moves bring people together in real life
- Shifts helps people navigate work, wages, red flags, and rights
- Leads provide trusted guidance without creating hierarchy-heavy power dynamics

## Product Guardrails

- fLOKr remains the product identity.
- RiseUp is a prototype/source of ideas, not a visible brand in fLOKr UI.
- Backend model names may remain stable while the product language evolves.
- The MVP should prioritize clarity over an oversized social network.
